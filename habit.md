# Privacy Policy for HabitTrack

**Effective date:** 29 October 2026
**Last updated:** 29 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how HabitTrack ("the app", "we", "us", "our"), developed by Aman Jeet Singh, collects, uses, and shares information when you use the HabitTrack Android app (package name `com.havit.habittrack`).

By using HabitTrack, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information you give us

| Data | When it is collected | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Name, email address** | When you create an account with email or sign in with Google | To create and secure your account |
| **Password** | When you sign up with email | Handled entirely by Firebase Authentication. We never see or store your password |
| **Habit data**: habit names, descriptions, schedules, reminder times, targets, completions, streaks, timer and count values | When you create and track habits | To provide the core habit-tracking features |
| **In-app credits and stakes**: credit balance, stake amounts, commitment levels, credits donated in the app | When you use credits, stakes, or the charity feature | To run the wallet, commitment, and charity features |
| **AI Habit Coach messages** (Prime only) | When you chat with the AI Habit Coach | To generate replies to your questions |
| **App settings**: theme, colours, notification preferences, cloud backup setting | When you change settings | To remember your preferences |

### 1.2 Information collected automatically

| Data | Collected by | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Advertising ID, device model, OS version, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, ad interactions** | Google AdMob (only for users who see ads) | To show and measure ads, and prevent ad fraud |
| **App usage events, device information, app instance ID** | Google Analytics for Firebase | To understand how the app is used and improve it |
| **App integrity signals** | Firebase App Check and Google Play Integrity | To confirm requests come from the genuine app and prevent abuse |
| **Purchase information**: product purchased, purchase token, order status | Google Play Billing | To unlock purchases and subscriptions |

We do **not** collect your precise location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or files.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

- Create and manage your account and keep it secure.
- Store and display your habits, streaks, progress, and reminders.
- Send habit reminders and alarm notifications that you set up.
- Back up and restore your habits through Cloud Backup (Prime feature).
- Provide AI Habit Coach replies based on your questions and habit progress (Prime feature).
- Process purchases and unlock Remove Ads or Prime features.
- Show ads to free users, and stop showing them to Remove Ads and Prime users.
- Analyse app usage to fix bugs and improve features.
- Prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorised access.

We do **not** sell your personal information.

---

## 3. Where Your Data Is Stored

- **On your device:** Habits, progress, and settings are stored locally on your phone.
- **In the cloud (Google Firebase):**
  - Your account (name, email) is stored in Firebase Authentication.
  - Your wallet and credit data is stored in Cloud Firestore when you are signed in.
  - If you are a Prime user and turn on **Cloud Backup**, your habits and habit history are also stored in Cloud Firestore so you can restore them on another device.

Firebase servers may be located outside your country, including in the United States. Google protects this data under its own security and privacy commitments.

---

## 4. AI Habit Coach

The AI Habit Coach is available to Prime subscribers. When you send a message:

- Your message, your recent chat messages in that session, and a **summary of your habits** (habit names, targets, schedules, streaks, and completion counts) are sent to **Google's Gemini models through Firebase AI Logic** to generate a reply.
- We do **not** send your name, email address, or account ID to the AI.
- Chat conversations are **not saved** by the app. They are cleared when you leave the chat screen.
- Google may process and temporarily store this data under the [Gemini API Terms](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Where the free tier of the Gemini API is used, Google may use submitted content to improve its products.
- Please **do not share sensitive personal information** (such as health conditions, financial details, or passwords) in the chat.
- AI replies can be inaccurate and are **not** medical, mental-health, legal, or financial advice.

## 6. Advertising

Free users see ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID to show personalised ads.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads at any time in **Android Settings → Google → Ads** (or **Settings → Privacy → Ads** on some devices). Users who buy **Remove Ads** or subscribe to **Prime** do not see ads.

---

## 7. Purchases and Subscriptions

All payments are processed by **Google Play**. We never receive or store your card, UPI, or other payment details. We only receive confirmation of what you purchased so we can unlock it.

Subscriptions (Prime Monthly and Prime Yearly) renew automatically until cancelled. You can manage or cancel them in the **Google Play Store → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions**.

**In-app credits** and **stake tokens** are virtual items used only inside the app. They have no cash value and cannot be withdrawn. "Donations" made with in-app credits are a motivational in-app feature and do not transfer real money unless explicitly stated in the app.

---

## 8. Notifications and Alarms

HabitTrack asks for permission to send notifications and schedule exact alarms so it can remind you about your habits at the times you choose. It also restarts your reminders after your phone reboots. You can turn reminders off in the app's settings or in Android Settings at any time.

---

## 9. Data Retention

- **Local data** stays on your device until you delete habits, clear app data, or uninstall the app.
- **Account and cloud data** is kept while your account is active.
- When you request account deletion, we delete your account, wallet, and cloud backup data within **30 days**, except where we must keep limited data to meet legal obligations.
- Google may keep analytics and advertising data according to its own retention settings. Firebase Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months by default.

---

## 10. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live (including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and the EU/UK GDPR), you may have the right to:

- Access the personal data we hold about you.
- Correct inaccurate data.
- Delete your account and data.
- Withdraw consent (for example, by turning off Cloud Backup, not using the AI Coach, or opting out of personalised ads).
- Raise a complaint with your local data protection authority.

**To delete your account and data**, email us at **[support email]** from the email address linked to your account, with the subject "Delete my HabitTrack account". We will confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days.

Signing out of the app does **not** delete your cloud data.

## 11. Children's Privacy

HabitTrack is not intended for children under **13** (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.
